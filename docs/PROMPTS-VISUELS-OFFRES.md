# Prompts visuels — Offres Made by Ischou

Ces prompts sont conçus pour créer des photos de produit **honnêtes sur le volume**. Ils doivent être utilisés avec la photo réelle du contenant take-away de la cliente comme image de référence. Les photos des grandes crêpes et pastels fournies le 27 août 2026 servent uniquement de repères d’échelle et de forme.

## Règle commune à conserver dans chaque génération

```text
Use the supplied real take-away packaging reference for the exact white hinged clamshell shape and material. The food must look generous, handmade and substantial, never miniature, bite-size or toy-like. Every announced piece must be visible and individually countable. Warm-brown wooden tabletop, natural diffused window light from the left, soft realistic shadows, professional 50mm food photography, three-quarter slightly elevated angle, high detail, appetizing but honest.

No text, logo, watermark, labels, printed packaging, hands, people, fingers, cutlery, sauce cups, decorative herbs, confetti, artificial steam, cartoon, 3D render, plastic shine, HDR, oversaturation or clutter.
```

## Box de crêpes

| Produit | Prompt spécifique |
|---|---|
| Petite Box Vanille — 7 pièces | `Show exactly SEVEN long, generous French-style vanilla crêpes. Each crêpe is rolled into a substantial cylinder and must be clearly countable one by one. The box looks comfortably filled by long crêpes, never short mini-rolls. A restrained ivory vanilla filling may be visible at one or two open ends. No chocolate, banana, strawberries, other fruit or whipped cream.` |
| Petite Box Chocolat — 6 pièces | `Show exactly SIX long, generous French-style chocolate crêpes. Each crêpe is rolled into a substantial cylinder and must be clearly countable one by one. Include only a restrained thin dark chocolate drizzle and a little chocolate filling visible at one open end. No banana, strawberries, other fruit or whipped cream.` |
| Petite Box Chocolat-Banane — 6 pièces | `Show exactly SIX long, generous French-style chocolate-banana crêpes. Each crêpe is rolled into a substantial cylinder and must be clearly countable one by one. Include a modest dark chocolate drizzle and a few fresh banana slices only as part of this approved flavour. No strawberries, other fruit or whipped cream.` |
| Box Classique Vanille — 10 pièces | `Show exactly TEN long, generous French-style vanilla crêpes. Arrange them in two neat readable rows with only modest overlap, so all ten pieces remain distinguishable. The box must look genuinely full, but each crêpe remains long and substantial. ## Prompt complet copiable

Copiez la règle commune, puis ajoutez le prompt spécifique du produit. Choisissez un cadrage `4:3` pour les cards de la landing page. Après génération, vérifiez manuellement la quantité visible avant de publier l’image : 7, 6, 6, 10, 9, 9 pour les box ; 5, 5, 5, 11, 11, 11 pour les formules pastels.
